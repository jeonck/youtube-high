- **근거:** VPH 15,970으로 초급상승 기준(1만)을 크게 상회하며, 'I TESTED X MYTHS IN Y' 포맷은 소재만 바꾸면 AI 툴·생활 꿀팁 분야에서 1인 제작 쇼츠로 즉시 재현 가능한 챌린지 포맷임
- **액션:** ChatGPT·Claude 등 AI 툴에 대한 '흔한 오해 5가지를 직접 테스트' 쇼츠 제작 — 후킹: '다들 이 AI 기능 안 된다고 하는데 직접 해봤습니다' + 결과를 PASS/FAIL 자막으로 빠르게 보여주는 편집 포맷 적용
```text
Everyone says these AI features are overhyped. I tested five of the most common myths so you do not have to. Myth one: ChatGPT can write a full business plan in two minutes. TRUE. I timed it myself. Myth two: AI can edit your photos with just a text prompt. TRUE. Changed the background, fixed the lighting, all in seconds. Myth three: AI-written code actually runs without errors. MOSTLY FALSE. It works about seven out of ten times, but it still saves hours of work. Myth four: AI can summarize an hour-long video in thirty seconds. TRUE. Tested it on a lecture and got a clean five-point summary. The tools people say are useless are actually wild right now. Follow so you catch the next test before everyone else does.
```
