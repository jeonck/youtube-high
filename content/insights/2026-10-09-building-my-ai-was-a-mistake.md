```text
I handed my entire schedule to an AI. It was the worst decision I made this year.

I used AI to manage my emails, plan my day, and write my content. For a while, it felt like a superpower.

Then it quietly canceled a meeting I needed. It replied to a client in completely the wrong tone. It wrote content that said all the right words but felt completely empty.

Here is what no one warns you about. AI does not know when it is wrong. It just keeps going, confidently, straight into disaster.

The real mistake was trusting AI like a decision maker instead of using it like a tool. The moment you stop checking its work, it stops working for you.

Learn from my fail before you make the same one. Follow for honest AI lessons that actually save you time.
```
