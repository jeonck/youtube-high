Everyone knows AI could be dangerous. So why are we building it faster than ever? Here is the dark truth. Every major tech company is in a race right now. If one slows down, another takes the lead. And no one wants to be left behind. It is not that the people building AI do not see the cliff. They see it. But stopping means losing. And losing feels worse than the risk. Economists call this a coordination problem. Everyone would be safer if we all slowed down. But no one can afford to be the first to stop. So we keep sprinting. All of us. Toward the edge.
