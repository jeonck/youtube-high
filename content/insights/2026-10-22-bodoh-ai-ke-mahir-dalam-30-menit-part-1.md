```text
Most people use AI every day and still get trash results. Here is why. You are skipping three lines that change everything. Line one, give AI a role. Say you are an expert in blank before your question. Line two, state your goal and what you already know. Line three, tell it the exact format you want. Bullet points. A table. One short paragraph. That is it. Without these three lines, AI guesses what you need and usually guesses wrong. With them, your output sounds like it came from an actual specialist, not a generic chatbot. I tested this across ten different prompts and the difference was immediate. Try it right now on whatever you were about to ask. Follow so you never waste a prompt again.
```
