AI is not saving your time. It is stealing your decisions. Marketing legend Seth Godin just issued a warning. AI gives you infinite options, infinite rewrites, infinite attempts. That sounds like freedom. But it is a trap. Because the people who win with AI are not the ones who use it the most. They are the ones who know when to stop. When to commit. When to ship.
