Mathematicians are furious at AI, and here is exactly why. AI tools like ChatGPT keep solving math problems with completely wrong answers, but with total confidence. That is the real problem. AI does not actually do math. It predicts what a correct answer should look like based on text patterns. So when it faces a new type of problem, it just makes up an answer, and it sounds absolutely sure of itself. Researchers have even caught AI claiming to prove theorems that were already disproven decades ago. This is called hallucination, and it is one of the biggest unsolved problems in AI right now. So the next time you trust AI with numbers, double check everything. If top mathematicians cannot trust it, neither should you. Follow for more AI truths you actually need to know.
