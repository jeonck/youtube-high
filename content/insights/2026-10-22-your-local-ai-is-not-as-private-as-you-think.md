You installed a local AI to keep your data private. But here is what no one told you. Local AI apps are not automatically safe. Many send telemetry even when running offline. Some tools quietly log your prompts and sync them to the cloud without clear warnings. Model update processes often require internet access and can include hidden data transfers. Even fully offline models may expose data through file system permissions or memory access. Before trusting any local AI with sensitive information, check its network activity, read the privacy policy carefully, and monitor your firewall logs. The safest local AI is one you actually audit first. Save this before your next AI session.
