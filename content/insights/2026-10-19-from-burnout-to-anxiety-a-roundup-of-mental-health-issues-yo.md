You think you are just tired. But what if it is not tiredness at all. Burnout and anxiety feel almost identical, but they need completely different solutions. With burnout, you feel empty, numb, like nothing matters anymore. With anxiety, your brain will not stop. You feel wired but exhausted at the same time. Here is the key difference. Burnout comes from doing too much for too long. Anxiety comes from fearing what might happen next. Treating them the same way is why so many people stay stuck. If rest makes you feel more guilty, that is burnout. If rest makes your mind race even faster, that is anxiety. Knowing which one you have is the first step to actually getting better. Save this and share it with someone who needs to hear it.
