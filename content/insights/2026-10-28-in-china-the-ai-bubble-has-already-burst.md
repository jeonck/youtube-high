Half of China's AI startups have already vanished. And no one is talking about it.

Two years ago, China was pouring billions into AI. The government pushed. Investors rushed in. Everyone said China would lead the world.

But then growth stopped. Valuations collapsed. Tens of thousands of AI workers lost their jobs. Companies that raised hundreds of millions just quietly shut down.

Why? Too many startups chasing the same idea with no real way to make money. When government funding slowed, the whole system cracked.

China's AI bubble has already burst. And the big question now is whether the same thing is coming for the US and the rest of the world.
