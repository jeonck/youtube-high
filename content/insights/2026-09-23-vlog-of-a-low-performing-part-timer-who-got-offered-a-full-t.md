I was the worst part-timer at my job. My manager barely looked at me. My coworkers thought I would quit in a week. Then something shifted on day fifteen. I stopped trying to look busy. I picked one small task nobody else wanted, and I owned it every single day. Showed up early. Asked questions. Took notes. Not to impress anyone. Just to actually understand the work. On week three, my manager pulled me aside. I was sure I was getting let go. Instead, she handed me a full-time contract. Nobody expected that. Least of all me. The lesson? You do not need to be the best from the start. You just need to be the most consistent person in the room. Save this if you are grinding through a rough patch right now.
